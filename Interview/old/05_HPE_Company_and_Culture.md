## PART 1 — The Company

### The basics
- **Full name:** Hewlett Packard Enterprise. **Ticker:** HPE (NYSE).
- **Formed:** November 2015, when the original Hewlett-Packard Company split into two — HPE (enterprise hardware, services, networking) and HP Inc. (PCs and printers). Don't confuse the two.
- **Headquarters:** Spring, Texas, USA (Houston area). Moved there from California.
- **Global reach:** Americas, Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific.
- **Self-description:** the "edge-to-cloud" company; more recently, "a leader in essential enterprise technology bringing together AI, cloud, and networking."

### Leadership
- **CEO & President:** Antonio Neri — has led HPE since February 2018.
- **CFO:** Marie Myers.
- (Knowing the CEO's name is a small but real signal you did your homework.)

### ⭐ The Juniper acquisition — the #1 thing to know
- HPE acquired **Juniper Networks for ~$14 billion** — announced January 2024, closed July 2025. It's the **largest acquisition in HPE's history.**
- The US Department of Justice sued to block it (arguing too much power in wireless networking) before the matter was settled.
- It **transformed HPE** from a company seen as a legacy server maker into a networking- and AI-infrastructure powerhouse.
- HPE Networking now combines the older **Aruba** business with Juniper's assets (including **Mist AI** and **Apstra** software) and makes up roughly **30% of sales.**

### How the business is doing (as of mid-2026)
- Most recent quarter (reported June 1, 2026): **record revenue ~$10.7 billion, up ~40% year over year**; networking revenue ~$2.7B; AI systems orders ~$1.8B.
- Neri called the Juniper deal "a home run"; HPE said demand is strong enough to hit its fiscal-2028 targets two years early.
- The stock jumped sharply on that report — investors have re-rated HPE from a mature server vendor into an "AI stock."

### Business segments (five)
Server · Hybrid Cloud · Networking · Financial Services · Corporate Investments and Other.

### Key products to be able to name
- **Servers:** HPE ProLiant (rack/tower), HPE Synergy, Edgeline (edge).
- **HPC / Supercomputing:** HPE Cray EX and Cray XD (HPE builds some of the world's fastest supercomputers).
- **Storage:** HPE Alletra, HPE InfoSight.
- **Hybrid cloud / as-a-service:** **HPE GreenLake** — the flagship consumption-based (pay-per-use) platform; central to HPE's recurring-revenue strategy. *(This is the product covered in your Cloud guide.)*
- **Networking:** Aruba + Juniper (Mist AI, Apstra).
- **Virtualization:** HPE Morpheus (positioned as a VMware alternative).

### The strategy / narrative (the "Why HPE?" hook)
HPE's identity today rests on three pillars: **Networking, AI/High-Performance Computing, and Hybrid Cloud.** The unifying theme is being the **infrastructure that powers enterprise AI** — the "AI factory" — with a strong pivot toward high-margin networking and recurring "as-a-service" revenue (GreenLake).

### Main competitors
Cisco and Huawei (networking), Dell (servers), NetApp, Nutanix, Super Micro, and VMware (storage/cloud/virtualization).

## PART 2 — Working Culture in India

### Locations
HPE India offices: **Bengaluru** (the main engineering hub — Whitefield/Mahadevapura), **Chennai**, Noida, Gurugram, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, and Pune.

### Overall sentiment
- Company-wide Glassdoor rating ~**4.0/5**, with about **80% recommending** the company.
- Work-life balance ~**4.2/5**, culture and values ~**4.2/5**.
- Bangalore specifically: work-life balance ~**4.3/5**, ~**89% recommend.**

### What people consistently praise
- **Work-life balance** — the standout strength; flexible schedules, understanding management, generally low day-to-day pressure.
- **Learning & development** — strong training, certifications, and exposure; repeatedly called a great place to *start a career*.
- **Collaborative, supportive culture** — not micromanaged, open to sharing ideas, good colleagues and mentors.
- **Job security & stability** — valued especially by freshers.
- **Supportive environment for women employees** — comes up frequently.
- **Perks** — cab/transport facility, decent benefits, hybrid/flexible model, "Work Fits Your Life" program (parental leave + flexible arrangements).

### The honest tradeoffs
- **Pay & promotions:** compensation rated lower (~3.6/5); commonly described as below top-tier market rates, with slow hikes/promotions. Common advice: negotiate well *at joining*.
- **Big-company friction:** bureaucracy and slow decision-making; periodic restructuring/reorganizations; occasional resource constraints.
- **Job-security views are mixed** — freshers often cite strong security; others note restructuring risk (common across big tech).

### ⭐ The biggest theme: it depends on your manager
Across every review source, the single most repeated point is that your experience — work-life balance, WFH flexibility, growth — varies a lot by **team and manager**. The company sets a good baseline, but your specific team shapes the day-to-day.

## PART 3 — How to Use This in the Interview

**For "Why HPE / why join this company?":**
Combine the exciting company story with the culture that fits a strong start:
> *"HPE is at an exciting inflection point — the Juniper acquisition and the pivot to AI infrastructure and GreenLake's as-a-service model mean it's reinventing itself, not standing still. At the same time, it's known for strong mentorship, learning, and a collaborative culture, which is exactly the environment I want to grow in early in my career."*

**Do:**
- Mention the CEO (Antonio Neri), the Juniper deal, and GreenLake naturally.
- Tie your interest to hybrid cloud / AI infrastructure (connects to your Cloud guide).
- Frame culture around *growth and learning*, not just "good work-life balance."

**Don't:**
- Lead with "great work-life balance" as your main reason — it can read as wanting to coast.
- Confuse HPE with HP Inc. (printers/PCs).
- Quote stale financial figures — refresh the latest quarter before you go in.
